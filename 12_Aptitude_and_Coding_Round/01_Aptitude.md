## Solved examples (2 per topic)

### Speed, Time, Distance
1. A car travels 120 km at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h. Average speed? → 2·60·40/(100) = **48 km/h**.
2. A train 150 m long at 54 km/h crosses a pole in? → 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s → 150/15 = **10 s**.

### Trains / Relative speed
1. Two trains 100 m and 150 m long run in opposite directions at 40 and 50 km/h. Time to cross each other? → relative speed 90 km/h = 25 m/s → 250/25 = **10 s**.
2. Same direction at 60 and 40 km/h, lengths 120 m and 80 m → relative speed 20 km/h = 50/9 m/s → 200 ÷ (50/9) = **36 s**.

### Partnership
1. A invests ₹20,000 for 12 months, B ₹30,000 for 6 months. Profit ₹14,000 → ratio 240000 : 180000 = 4:3 → A **₹8,000**, B **₹6,000**.
2. A and B invest in ratio 3:5. A withdraws half his capital after 6 months. Annual profit ₹2,700 → A: 3·6 + 1.5·6 = 27; B: 5·12 = 60 → 27:60 = 9:20 → A = 2700·9/29 ≈ **₹838**.

### Arithmetic Progression
1. Sum of the first 20 terms of 3, 7, 11, … → a = 3, d = 4 → 20/2 × (6 + 76) = **820**.
2. A person saves ₹100 in month 1 and ₹20 more each month. Savings in month 12? → 100 + 11·20 = **₹320**; total = 12/2 × (200 + 220) = **₹2,520**.

### Percentage / Profit
1. Price up 20% then down 20% → net 20 − 20 − 400/100 = **−4%**.
2. CP ₹800, SP ₹1,000 → profit **25%**.

### Interest (banking flavour!)
1. SI on ₹50,000 at 8% for 3 years → **₹12,000**.
2. CI on ₹10,000 at 10% for 2 years → 10000 × 1.21 = 12,100 → CI **₹2,100**.

### Time & Work
1. A in 10 days, B in 15 → together 150/25 = **6 days**.
2. A + B in 12 days, A alone in 20 → B: 1/12 − 1/20 = 1/30 → **30 days**.

### Geometry
1. A circle with circumference 44 cm (π = 22/7) → r = 7 → area = **154 cm²**.
2. A right triangle with legs 6 and 8 → hypotenuse **10**, area **24**.

### Data Interpretation (typical)
Loans by branch (₹ cr): BLR 400, MUM 600, DEL 500, CHN 300.
1. MUM's share of the total? → 600/1800 = **33.3%**.
2. If BLR grows 25% next year and CHN 50%, which is bigger? → BLR 500 vs CHN 450 → **BLR**.

### Logical reasoning (common)
1. Series 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ? → differences 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 → **42**.
2. If CODE = DPEF, then BANK = ? → each letter +1 → **CBOL**.
