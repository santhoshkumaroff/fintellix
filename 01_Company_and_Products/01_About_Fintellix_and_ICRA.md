## Why ICRA bought Fintellix (good line for "why join us?")
ICRA is strong in **credit risk know-how** (ratings, research). Fintellix brings the **technology platform** that turns bank data into regulator-ready reports. Together they can sell an integrated "risk + regulatory + data" solution. ICRA's CEO said the space is "fast evolving with increasing regulatory scrutiny." In ICRA's May 2026 results, the analytics segment grew year on year, "driven by acquisition of Fintellix."

## Interview questions about the company

### Q1. What do you know about Fintellix?
**Answer:** "Fintellix is a Bengaluru-based RegTech company, founded in 2006 and part of Group ICRA since October 2025. It builds a data platform and pre-built solutions that help banks collect data from their source systems, clean and validate it, compute risk metrics, and submit regulatory returns to central banks such as RBI, CBUAE, SAMA, MAS, HKMA, APRA and the Bank of Mauritius. It also builds SupTech platforms that regulators use to collect data from banks. Recently it has added AI features, such as **Finbot**, a conversational assistant that answers data questions through SQL, and an AI-driven Compliance Tracker."
- **Example 1:** A bank in Kuwait must file monthly returns to the Central Bank of Kuwait. Fintellix's CBK solution pulls loans and deposits from the core banking system, applies the regulator's rules and produces the return in the required format.
- **Example 2:** A regulator like the Bank of Mauritius can use Fintellix's Supervisory Data Collection system to receive, validate and analyse data from all banks under it.

### Q2. What is RegTech? What is SupTech?
**Answer:** RegTech (regulatory technology) is software that helps **regulated firms** (banks, insurers) comply with rules: reporting, risk calculation, compliance tracking. SupTech (supervisory technology) is software that helps **regulators** supervise those firms. Fintellix does both.
- **Example 1 (RegTech):** Automating RBI's monthly NPA return for a bank, instead of 10 people building it in Excel.
- **Example 2 (SupTech):** A central bank dashboard that flags which banks' liquidity ratios are falling.

### Q3. Why do you want to join Fintellix?
**Answer structure:** (1) domain, (2) GenAI on real data, (3) learning, (4) stability.
"Three reasons. First, regulatory reporting is a problem where accuracy really matters: one wrong number goes to the central bank. That makes GenAI work here more serious and more interesting than a demo chatbot. Second, the JD says I'll be on live projects from the first weeks, building RAG and LLM automation with Claude, which is exactly what I've been teaching myself. Third, being part of ICRA, with Moody's as its largest shareholder, means I'd learn credit-risk and banking from people who rate banks for a living."
- **Example 1:** "I read that Finbot answers data questions using SQL. I'd love to work on making that kind of tool accurate and auditable."
- **Example 2:** "In my project I built document Q&A. RBI circulars are a perfect real-world corpus for that."

### Q4. Who are Fintellix's customers?
**Answer:** Banks and financial institutions in India, the Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait), Asia-Pacific (Singapore, Hong Kong, Philippines, Thailand, South Korea, Australia), Africa (Mauritius), the UK (including Lloyd's syndicate reporting for insurers) and the USA (CECL/CCAR). Regulators are also customers (SupTech).
- **Example 1:** An Indian private bank using Fintellix for RBI returns and risk-based supervision data.
- **Example 2:** A Lloyd's insurance syndicate in London using Fintellix's pre-configured Syndicate Reporting to generate and validate Lloyd's returns.

### Q5. Who are their competitors and how is Fintellix different?
**Answer:** Competitors include Regnology (Germany), IRIS Business Services (India), CUBE and Corlytics (regulatory intelligence). Fintellix's differentiators, from its website: **platform approach** (one data model reused across many use cases and countries), **"codeless" conversion** from a standardised schema to many regulatory formats, cloud-first microservices architecture, and **holistic engagement** (product + implementation + change management + managed services).
- **Example 1:** A bank operating in India and the UAE can feed one standard data model and generate both RBI and CBUAE returns.
- **Example 2:** When a regulator changes a template, business users reconfigure it through the UI instead of waiting for code changes.

### Q6. What is ICRA? What is the Moody's connection?
**Answer:** ICRA Limited is one of India's leading credit rating agencies. It rates the creditworthiness of companies and debt instruments. Moody's Investors Service is its largest shareholder. Fintellix is now part of ICRA's Research & Analytics vertical, alongside ICRA Analytics and D2K Technologies.
- **Example 1:** ICRA assigns a rating like "[ICRA]AA" to a company's bonds, which tells investors how risky they are.
- **Example 2:** ICRA Analytics provides research, modelling and data services. Fintellix adds the regulatory reporting technology.

### Q7. What is a "Group ICRA company"? Does it change anything for interns?
**Answer:** It means Fintellix is a subsidiary of ICRA. For you it means access to domain experts in credit risk, a stable and listed parent, and likely more cross-selling of AI-driven risk products, which means more GenAI work.

### Q8. Do you know about any AI product Fintellix has?
**Answer:** Yes. Their solution sheets list **Finbot**, a conversational AI chatbot that answers data questions by generating SQL queries, and an **AI-driven Compliance Tracker** for regulatory change management. The JD also says interns will build GenAI apps with prompt engineering, Claude and RAG, and evaluate their accuracy.
- **Example 1 (Finbot style):** A risk officer types "Show top 10 borrowers by overdue amount in Karnataka". The bot generates SQL, runs it and returns a table.
- **Example 2 (Compliance Tracker style):** When RBI publishes a new circular, the AI reads it, summarises what changed, and maps it to the affected reports and owners.
